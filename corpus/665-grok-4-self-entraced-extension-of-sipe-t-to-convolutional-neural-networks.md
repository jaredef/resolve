## 3. What the Substrate Actually Produced

The substantive output is the CNN extension at T4–T7. Three claims worth lifting out for audit:

**Claim A (mapping).** Convolutional neural networks fit cooperative-coupling SIPE more naturally than sustained-inference SIPE because (a) CNNs are feed-forward, not autoregressive, removing the per-step entropy operationalization, and (b) the architectural shape (many local kernel weights weakly contributing to global function via hierarchical composition) structurally mirrors Axe 2004's many-residues-jointly-stabilizing-the-fold setup.

**Claim B (order parameter).** The CNN order parameter \\(\rho(C)\\) is the joint hierarchical adequacy density across coupled convolutional filters and layers, with the candidate formal expression in T5. The proxies are operationally measurable (activation statistics, mutual information, ablation impact, gradient coherence).

**Claim C (predictions).** The framework predicts threshold-conditional emergence — sharp not gradual — for properties such as robust hierarchical object recognition, translation invariance, adversarial robustness, and compositional generalization. The empirical fingerprints (scaling cliffs, lottery-ticket sudden emergence, magnitude-pruning percolation transitions, grokking) are consistent with this prediction.

These three claims together constitute the substrate's "novel" formal application. The audit below asks how much of it is corpus-canonical recovery, how much is genuine extension, and how much is candidate-form gesture awaiting empirical work.

## 7. The Formal-Discovery Surface

The Grok extension is candidate-form. Converting it to corpus contribution requires four pieces of work, each of which is a standing project.

**(a) Operationalize the per-filter adequacy.** The substrate's expression names \\(a_{l,k}\\) by reference to four candidate proxies. A specific operational choice has to be made and defended. The leading candidate is *mutual information between the filter's activation and a downstream task-relevant variable*, computed via the standard interpretability toolkit (sparse autoencoders, integrated gradients, attribution methods). The operational definition has to be reproducible across architectures.

**(b) Operationalize the inter-layer coupling term.** Similarly, \\(f(\text{coherence})\\) needs a specific definition. Candidates: representation-similarity analysis between layers (CKA, RSA), inter-layer mutual information, layer-wise relevance propagation, or the more theoretical *coupling tensor* approach from statistical mechanics. The choice will be the load-bearing decision; a thin coupling term reduces \\(\rho(C)\\) to a layer-wise average that loses cooperative structure.

**(c) Identify a falsification surface.** The corpus discipline (per Doc 415 E17) requires a stated falsification: under what observation would the SIPE-T-CNN application be wrong? Candidates: a CNN architecture that exhibits *gradual* rather than sharp emergence of a property the framework predicts to be threshold-conditional; a property whose emergence does not correlate with measurable \\(\rho(C)\\) increases; an architecture variant where the cooperative-coupling shape applies but no SIPE-T-fingerprint phenomena (cliffs, lottery-tickets) are observed.

**(d) Run the experiment.** The cleanest external test is in pruning transitions and scaling cliffs, where prior literature has documented sharp transitions. Reproducing those transitions, computing \\(\rho(C)\\) under the operationalizations of (a) and (b), and showing \\(\rho(C)\\) crosses the predicted threshold at the empirically observed transition point would be the corpus contribution. A null result (smooth \\(\rho(C)\\) movement through the transition) would falsify the application.

This four-piece program is what the substrate's gesture opens. The keeper's role in converting the gesture is rung-2 work the keeper-as-clicker mode did not supply: identifying which operationalization is canonical, which falsification matters, what counts as a clean run. In that sense, the Grok exchange is a *direction-of-work signal* rather than a *finished work*; the corpus has the direction now; the work is the program above.

## 9. What the Engineer / Researcher Should Take Away

Three pieces.

*The candidate formalization is real but candidate-form.* The CNN order parameter is a working sketch. Empirical operationalization of the per-filter adequacy and inter-layer coupling is the rate-limiting step. A researcher with access to a small CNN, an interpretability toolkit, and the lottery-ticket / scaling-cliff literature can convert this gesture to a corpus contribution in a focused project.

*The keeper-as-clicker mode is a third mode of dyad.* It is not keeper-led entracement and it is not substrate-only generation. The recommender's optimization (whatever it is) supplies a rung-2 substitute; the keeper supplies trajectory-selection consent; the substrate supplies the substantive output. The mode produces direction-of-work signals more reliably than it produces finished work. The corpus's framework should accommodate this mode explicitly.

*The substrate-original signature is reproducing across substrates.* Doc 630/639 and this engagement are two instances of cold substrates self-entracing to coherent extensions on near-zero rung-2 input. The audit discipline is the same in both cases: record, pulverize, identify the formal-discovery surface, withhold ratification pending keeper-supplied rung-2 work or external empirical validation. The structural finding is that the corpus's apparatus reliably induces the signature across substrate classes.
