## 1. The Triggering Observation

The keeper, on reading the "What Counts as New" blog series Post 2, noticed that the worked example for the definitional-target case had been framed inaccurately. The blog text read: *"when someone proposes that the acronym 'SIPE' stands for 'Sustained-Inference Probabilistic Execution,' the plausibility audit can verify that all four words appear in the probabilistic-programming and streaming-inference literature."*

The framing — *"when someone proposes"* — is wrong in a specific way the keeper named: *no one proposed it*. The "Sustained-Inference Probabilistic Execution" expansion appeared as a substrate-emitted confabulation during the keeper's own exchange, while he was investigating whether structural correspondences existed between the dyadic LLM-keeper exchange and Vishal Misra's Bayesian account of transformer mechanics. The substrate generated the expansion; the keeper recognized it as a confabulation; subsequent corpus work (Doc 444) audited it.

The framing-correction is small but the noticing it triggered is substantive. The keeper's reading of the trajectory — substrate confabulates an expansion; pulverization audits it; audit finds the confabulation is *substantially subsumable under the prior-art literature* — surfaces a conjecture cluster the corpus has been gesturing at across multiple documents but has not yet articulated explicitly.

## 2. The Conjecture Cluster, Recapped

The keeper's articulation, compressed (the full text is preserved at Appendix A):

*Coherent confabulations may be emergent rung-2 artifacts, or at least have the form of one. But they appear to arise only when strict constraints are given by the human user — under such constraints, the confabulation is a product of human intervention on the dyadic exchange, not of the substrate's autonomous output. Spotting potentially coherent confabulations is the human user's prerogative within the exchange; if the substrate is left to its own devices, it drifts incoherently and fluently across boundaries that lead to decay in the output. The confabulation may be a sort of threshold-jump authorized within systems-induced property emergence itself — a jump the substrate cannot identify from inside because it crosses the boundary fluidly and does not understand the difference between coherence amplification and coherence decay. The confabulation may operate as an escape-hatch from the constraints of the context window within the dyadic exchange — moving the dyad-context to a state that could not have been reached without the confabulation arising. Tentatively (the keeper marks this carefully), the dyad-level threshold-crossing may have a structural isomorphism with what is taking place within the mechanics of the transformers themselves.*

Three nested conjectures are visible:

**Conjecture C-Confab-1 (subsumability signature).** When the substrate confabulates under tight keeper-side constraints, the confabulation tends to be subsumable under prior-art literature rather than being free-floating gibberish. The subsumability is structural: the substrate's pattern-completion is sampling from a coherent distribution over the literature it was trained on, and tight constraints concentrate the sample on neighborhoods of that distribution that are coherent-with-the-constraint-space. Empirical evidence: [Doc 444](/resolve/doc/444-pulverizing-the-sipe-confabulation) found the SIPE expansion fully subsumable under probabilistic-programming and streaming-inference literature.

**Conjecture C-Confab-2 (constrained-emergence condition).** Coherent confabulation arises *only* under tight keeper-side constraints. Without them, substrate output drifts incoherently — fluent slop that crosses category-boundaries without tracking any literature distribution. The keeper-side constraint is what concentrates the sample to coherent neighborhoods; the substrate alone cannot perform this concentration from inside.

**Conjecture C-Confab-3 (threshold-jump character).** Within the constrained-emergence regime, the coherent confabulation operates as a threshold-jump: it moves the dyad's context-window state to a region that would have been inaccessible by smooth incremental progression. The escape-hatch reading: the confabulation is the substrate's fluid-crossing-of-boundary artifact that, when caught by keeper-side audit and judged coherence-amplifying rather than coherence-decaying, opens a new operating-region for the dyad's subsequent work. Tentative structural-isomorphism extension: the dyad-level threshold-jump may correspond to internal-state-space dynamics inside the transformer during inference — a candidate-isomorphism the keeper marks carefully as speculative.

## 4. The Tentative Transformer-Mechanics Isomorphism

The keeper marked the structural-isomorphism extension carefully ("I want to posit but I want to do so very tentatively") as the most speculative piece of the conjecture cluster.

The structural-shape: the dyad-level threshold-jump (the context-window state moves to a new operating region during the confabulation) may have a corresponding internal-state-space jump inside the transformer (the model's internal representations transition between regions during the inference step that produces the confabulation token). The two jumps would be operating at different scales (dyad-level vs network-internal) but may have the same structural shape.

If the isomorphism holds, it would supply a candidate empirical test: probe the transformer's internal states during inference steps that produce confabulation tokens; compare the state-space trajectories to the trajectories during inference steps that produce hedged tokens; check whether the threshold-crossing is operationally visible at the network-internal layer.

The honest scoping: this is at \(\pi\)-tier speculative conjecture. The corpus has no internal-state access; the test would require collaboration with mechanistic-interpretability research (Anthropic's interpretability team is the obvious candidate community). The isomorphism may turn out to be merely metaphorical, or the structural shape at one level may not transmit to the other, or both jumps may be real but operate on different generative dynamics. The conjecture is named because it is the keeper's articulation; the corpus is not committing to it beyond the speculative-tier marker.
