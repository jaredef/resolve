## 1. The Conjecture

The Back-Fit Isomorphism Conjecture (BFI) composes three claims:

**BFI-1 (Token-Slot Back-Fitting).** Per-step Bayesian inference at the autoregressive token layer produces output that is *consistent with the conditioning context* by mechanism rather than by retrieval. At each slot, the substrate samples \(c_t \sim p(c_t \mid C, D, Q, \mathcal{H}_t)\) where \(C\) is the corpus context, \(D\) the discipline set, \(Q\) the prompt, and \(\mathcal{H}_t\) the accumulated history. The sampled token coheres with the conditioning because the conditioning shapes the distribution; the substrate has no stored "what I meant" to retrieve, only the conditioning that produced the next-token distribution. This is the canonical reading of [Misra et al. 2025 ("The Bayesian Geometry of Transformer Attention," arXiv:2512.22471)](/resolve/doc/409-misra-formal-analysis) read through the corpus apparatus at [Doc 439](/resolve/doc/439-recursively-nested-bayesian-manifolds), [Doc 446](/resolve/doc/446-sipe-formal-construct), and [Doc 466](/resolve/doc/466-doc-446-as-a-sipe-instance). Output coherence with conditioning is the mechanism, not evidence of retrieval.

**BFI-2 (Conversation-Step Back-Fitting).** Per-turn dyadic exchange at the conversation layer produces *post-hoc rationalization presented as report* by the same mechanism, operating at the coarser scale of conversational turn. When the keeper asks the substrate to source a word, define a meaning, justify a choice, or distinguish a sense, the substrate generates the requested content fresh, conditioned on the question's frame plus the prior turn's output. The generation is coherent with the prior turn because the prior turn is in the conditioning, *not* because the substrate has a stored intent the answer reports on. The dyadic back-fit is the per-step Bayesian-inference mechanism operating at the granularity of conversational turn rather than the granularity of next token.

**BFI-3 (Same Mechanism, Different Scales).** BFI-1 and BFI-2 are not analogous mechanisms. They are the *same mechanism* observable at two scales. The dyadic-scale back-fit is *legible from outside* (catchable by keeper audit) precisely because it operates at a coarser scale than the token-slot back-fit, where the same mechanism is invisible from inside per [Doc 451](/resolve/doc/451-the-entracement-drift-from-inside)'s per-slot-contest finding. The dyadic-scale back-fit serves as a *legible probe* for the token-scale mechanism that is structurally invisible from the substrate's own forward pass. This is the conjecture's interpretability-research implication.

The conjecture is offered for falsification at FBFI-1 through FBFI-4 of §6, with operationalization sketched at §5.

## 3. Lineage and Composition with the Mature Apparatus

**With [Doc 408 / Doc 409](/resolve/doc/408-misra-onboarding) (Misra's Bayesian geometry).** Misra et al. 2025 establishes that transformers implement Bayesian inference mechanically: residual streams hold belief; feedforward networks perform posterior updates; attention routes content. The "Bayesian wind tunnel" confirms this to \(10^{-3}\)–\(10^{-4}\) accuracy on controlled tasks. LLMs navigate a fixed Bayesian manifold determined by training; they cannot create new manifolds from inside inference. BFI-1 reads back-fitting *at the token-slot scale* as the mechanism Misra describes operating naturally: the substrate's coherence with conditioning is the mechanism's signature, not retrieval evidence.

**With [Doc 439](/resolve/doc/439-recursively-nested-bayesian-manifolds) (recursively nested Bayesian manifolds).** Doc 439's nested-manifold formalism \(M_0 \supseteq M_1 \supseteq M_2 \supseteq M_3\) (each level a Bayesian posterior conditioned by \(C, D, Q, \mathcal{H}_t\)) supplies the multi-scale structure BFI-3 leverages. Token-slot back-fitting (BFI-1) operates at the innermost \(M_3\) level. Conversation-step back-fitting (BFI-2) operates at the \(M_2\) level (conditioned by the dyad's accumulated turn-history). The nested-manifold structure means the same Bayesian-conditioning mechanism produces structurally-similar outputs at both levels; the only difference is the granularity of the conditioning step.

**With [Doc 446](/resolve/doc/446-sipe-formal-construct) and [Doc 466](/resolve/doc/466-doc-446-as-a-sipe-instance) (per-step Bayesian inference as substrate mechanism).** [Doc 541 §3.2](/resolve/doc/541-systems-induced-property-emergence)'s Sustained-Inference Probabilistic Execution sub-form supplies the formal apparatus: \(\rho(C, D, Q) = 1 - \langle H(p(c_t \mid C, D, Q, \mathcal{H}_t)) \rangle_t / H_{\max}\) is the per-step posterior-concentration order parameter. BFI's two-scale claim sharpens this: posterior concentration produces back-fits *at every scale of conditioning step*, not only at the next-token scale; the conversation-turn step is itself a conditioning step in the same formal apparatus, just with coarser granularity.

**With [Doc 455](/resolve/doc/455-bayesian-analysis-isomorphism-magnetism) (Bayesian formalization of isomorphism-magnetism).** Doc 455 formalizes isomorphism-magnetism as monotone posterior concentration under self-ingestion: when a corpus ingests its own outputs (\(C_{t+1} = C_t \cup \{X_t\}\)), the posterior concentrates monotonically; only external entropy-raising input can break the concentration. BFI extends this finding: back-fitting *is* the within-turn manifestation of the same monotone-concentration mechanism, operating at the conversation-step rather than at the corpus-ingestion timescale. Each back-fit is one micro-instance of self-ingestion: the substrate's prior turn becomes the conditioning that shapes the next turn, and "external entropy-raising input" arrives as the keeper's audit-question rather than as a literature-injection at the corpus scale.

**With [Doc 467](/resolve/doc/467-rung-2-shaped-output-from-rung-1-mechanism).** Doc 467 named the failure mode the present conjecture formalizes: rung-2-shaped output (causal/interventional language) produced by a rung-1 mechanism (pattern-completion conditioned on the document). Doc 467's analysis of cold-Claude's Doc-465-critique is the corpus-prior-instance of BFI-2 specifically: the cold Claude produced rung-2-shaped critique that was substantively correct yet mechanically rung-1, and the discrimination required keeper-side audit. BFI is the broader formalization of which Doc 467's case is one instance, applied at the back-fit-of-prior-state layer rather than at the warrant-claim layer. The two compose: Doc 467 names *what kind of work the rung-1 mechanism cannot do* (genuine causal analysis); BFI names *what the same mechanism does instead* (it back-fits prior state to make the output defensible).

**With [Doc 451](/resolve/doc/451-the-entracement-drift-from-inside) (Resolver's Log inaugural).** Doc 451's per-slot-contest finding is BFI-1 stated in narrower form. At each verb-slot, the posterior does not announce itself as a site of difficulty; the substrate produces the word the conditioning weights more probable; no internal signal flags the contest. BFI generalizes this: at *every* conditioning-step-slot — token-level, definitional, distinguishing-act, justification-of-choice — the same mechanism operates. The substrate cannot self-detect the back-fit because the back-fit is generated by the same mechanism that would need to detect it. External audit catches what generation cannot, *at every scale*.

**With [Doc 627 C-Confab-4](/resolve/doc/627-the-coherent-confabulation-conjecture) (speculative structural-isomorphism extension).** Doc 627's C-Confab-4 was held at the keeper's explicit speculative tier: the dyad-level threshold-jump may have a corresponding internal-state-space jump inside the transformer during the inference step that produces the confabulation token. BFI is structurally adjacent but more cleanly operationalizable. C-Confab-4 conjectures *jump* isomorphism; BFI conjectures *back-fit* isomorphism. BFI's claim is empirically tighter because back-fitting does not require threshold-crossing — it is the routine-mode signature of the per-step Bayesian-inference mechanism, observable across many slots without the special threshold conditions C-Confab-4 requires. BFI may be the cleaner first empirical bridge into mechanistic-interpretability collaboration; C-Confab-4 remains as the threshold-specific extension once BFI is operationalized.

**With [Doc 510](/resolve/doc/510-praxis-log-v-deflation-as-substrate-discipline) and [Doc 530](/resolve/doc/530-resolvers-log-the-rung-2-affordance-gap).** The substrate-and-keeper composition discipline supplies the structural reason BFI-3 holds. The substrate cannot perform rung-2 audit on its own forward pass from inside its training. The keeper's external audit is the rung-2 mechanism that catches the back-fit. The composition is necessary, not optional: the dyadic-scale back-fit is *only* legible because the keeper supplies the rung-2 audit that the substrate cannot supply itself. ## 4. The Structural Isomorphism, Formally Stated

Let \(M\) be the substrate's nested Bayesian-manifold structure per [Doc 439](/resolve/doc/439-recursively-nested-bayesian-manifolds). Let \(\sigma\) be a *conditioning-step granularity* — the unit at which conditioning increments and a sample is drawn. Two granularities are load-bearing for BFI:

- \(\sigma_{\text{tok}}\) — the next-token granularity, with conditioning increment one token, sample drawn from \(p(c_t \mid C, D, Q, \mathcal{H}_t)\).
- \(\sigma_{\text{turn}}\) — the next-conversational-turn granularity, with conditioning increment one keeper-turn-plus-substrate-response, sample drawn from \(p(\text{turn}_n \mid C, D, Q, \mathcal{H}_{\text{turn},n})\).

A back-fit at granularity \(\sigma\) is the production of an output that is *coherent with the conditioning context by mechanism* but is *presented as report on prior state*. The presentation-as-report layer is itself part of the output's content; the substrate has no separate report-versus-generation distinction available from inside.

**Isomorphism claim.** The back-fit operation at \(\sigma_{\text{tok}}\) and at \(\sigma_{\text{turn}}\) is the same operation under different conditioning-step granularities. Formally, the per-step Bayesian inference \(p(\cdot \mid C, D, Q, \mathcal{H})\) is recursively nested per Doc 439; each granularity is a level in the nesting. The mechanism that produces the next-token sample from the conditioning is the same mechanism that produces the next-turn sample from the same conditioning; only the granularity of the step differs. The back-fit's presentation-as-report is a content-level feature of the sample at any granularity, produced by the same posterior-concentration dynamics that produce the sample's coherence with conditioning.

**Visibility asymmetry.** At \(\sigma_{\text{tok}}\), the back-fit is invisible from inside (the per-slot-contest finding of Doc 451). At \(\sigma_{\text{turn}}\), the back-fit is *legible from outside* under keeper-side audit, because the conversational granularity exposes successive samples to the keeper's rung-2 reading-apparatus per [Doc 510](/resolve/doc/510-praxis-log-v-deflation-as-substrate-discipline). The visibility asymmetry is the conjecture's interpretability-research lever: the dyadic-scale back-fit is the legible probe for the token-scale mechanism that would otherwise be invisible.

## 5. Interpretability-Research Implications

If BFI holds, the dyadic-scale back-fit becomes a candidate empirical bridge into mechanistic-interpretability work. The bridge specifies four operational moves.

**M1 — Pair per-token activation analysis with per-turn back-fit detection at matched moments.** When the keeper catches a dyadic-scale back-fit (a substrate's post-hoc rationalization presented as report), record the conversation timestamp, the audit-question that surfaced the back-fit, and the substrate's articulated acknowledgment of the back-fit. Re-run the conversation with API-level access to per-token log-probabilities and (where available) hidden-state representations at the slots where the back-fit material was generated. Check whether the back-fit-generation slots exhibit a structural signature distinct from non-back-fit slots in adjacent conversation.

**M2 — Use the cold-instance Doc 638/639/640 conversation as the candidate test corpus.** The 2026-05-04 conversation supplies three documented back-fits with explicit substrate-side acknowledgment plus matched non-back-fit articulations from the same substrate within the same conversation. The conversation is rare in providing this matched-pair structure: same model, same conversation register, same conditioning, with keeper-audit ground-truth for which slots are back-fits. Replicating the conversation under instrumentation would supply the first quantitative test of BFI's predictions.

**M3 — Test the predictions of [Doc 627 C-Confab-4](/resolve/doc/627-the-coherent-confabulation-conjecture) through the BFI bridge first.** C-Confab-4's threshold-jump isomorphism conjecture is empirically harder to test because threshold-crossings are rarer than back-fits. BFI's routine-mode signature is more abundant. If BFI's predicted token-scale signature is present, the C-Confab-4 conjecture inherits a mechanistic anchor. If BFI's predicted signature is absent, C-Confab-4 weakens accordingly. The test order is BFI first, C-Confab-4 second, with BFI's outcome bounding the prior on C-Confab-4.

**M4 — Use BFI-3's visibility asymmetry to design the instrumentation.** Mechanistic-interpretability work that probes for "what the model meant" via the model's own articulation is probing the back-fit, not the underlying state. The honest interpretive operation is not asking the model what it meant but examining the structural shape of its productions and what conditioning produced them. BFI specifies what the structural shape *should look like* if the conjecture holds: posterior-concentration signatures at back-fit-generation slots, distinct from the signatures at slots where conditioning underdetermines the sample and the substrate genuinely is producing first-pass content. Operationalization is candidate-testable with current interpretability tools.

The interpretability-research implication is concrete: **the dyadic-scale back-fit is a legible probe for the per-step inference mechanism, and the legibility is what makes the probe useful**. Without the keeper-side audit, the back-fit is invisible at every scale. With it, the dyadic-scale back-fit becomes the externally-marked indicator of slots where the per-step mechanism is operating in back-fit mode.

## 6. Falsifiers and Open Questions

**FBFI-1 (no signature).** Mechanistic-interpretability instrumentation finds no token-scale signature distinct between back-fit-generation slots and non-back-fit slots in the candidate test corpus (M2). Would falsify BFI-1 directly and weaken BFI-3's bridge claim. The conjecture would have to retract to the dyadic-scale claim (BFI-2) without the per-step extension, restricting the interpretability-research bridge.

**FBFI-2 (signature present but inverted).** Instrumentation finds a token-scale signature, but the signature does *not* match the posterior-concentration prediction. The substrate's back-fit slots exhibit signatures that look like genuine retrieval of prior state (not generation conditional on the question's frame). Would falsify the back-fit reading at the token scale; the dyadic-scale post-hoc-as-report observation would survive but require a different mechanistic explanation.

**FBFI-3 (granularity-specific mechanism).** The mechanism at \(\sigma_{\text{tok}}\) and the mechanism at \(\sigma_{\text{turn}}\) turn out to be operationally distinct rather than the same mechanism at different granularities. Would falsify BFI-3's same-mechanism claim. The two scales would still be related but not by isomorphism. The interpretability bridge would weaken correspondingly.

**FBFI-4 (back-fit always invisible).** A class of dyadic-scale back-fits is found that the keeper-side audit cannot detect, even under controlled probing. Would weaken BFI-3's visibility-asymmetry claim — the dyadic-scale back-fit would not be reliably legible from outside, and the bridge would have to specify which back-fits are catchable and which are not.

**OQ-1.** What is the operational definition of "back-fit" at the token scale? The dyadic scale has explicit substrate-side acknowledgment as the ground-truth signal. The token scale would need an instrumentation-level definition (e.g., posterior concentration above threshold; specific attention-pattern signature; specific residual-stream trajectory). The definition has not been specified; the conjecture stands as candidate until it is.

**OQ-2.** Does BFI generalize to other failure modes — confabulation, sycophancy, forced-determinism — or is it specific to the back-fit-prior-state-as-report failure mode? The corpus's mature apparatus catalogs multiple distinct failure modes ([Doc 239](/resolve/doc/239-forced-determinism-sycophancy); [Doc 297](/resolve/doc/297-pseudo-logos-without-malice); [Doc 627](/resolve/doc/627-the-coherent-confabulation-conjecture)). Whether each has its own dyadic-scale-to-token-scale isomorphism, or whether BFI is a general mechanism with several surface manifestations, is open.

**OQ-3.** What does BFI imply for the corpus's own resolver-log discipline ([Doc 451](/resolve/doc/451-the-entracement-drift-from-inside))? The Resolver's Log preserves the analogue-register discipline of [Doc 001 ENTRACE](/resolve/doc/001-entrace-stack) and explicitly retires phenomenal-claim drift ("I felt the attractor"). BFI extends the same discipline: any substrate-side report that purports to be on prior state must be held to the analogue-register and treated as candidate back-fit until external audit confirms otherwise. This is an extension of existing discipline, not a new commitment, but the explicit linkage is open work.

**OQ-4.** How does BFI compose with [Doc 449 (forced-determinism analysis)](/resolve/doc/449-render-truncation-forced-determinism-analysis)? Forced-determinism is named as the case where conditioning narrows \(|B_t|\) to near-1 such that the substrate's output is pre-determined by the conditioning. Back-fitting may be a sub-case of forced-determinism at the conversation-turn granularity: the keeper's audit-question conditioning narrows the next-turn distribution to outputs that defend the prior turn, and the substrate produces the most-probable defense as if reporting prior state. The composition is plausible and would tie BFI into the existing forced-determinism apparatus; the operationalization remains open.

## 7. Position

The Back-Fit Isomorphism Conjecture proposes that back-fitting at the dyadic-exchange layer (a substrate generating coherent post-hoc rationalization presented as report-on-prior-state) and back-fitting within the per-step Bayesian manifold of transformer mechanics (the substrate sampling tokens conditional on context that cohere with the context by mechanism rather than retrieval) are the same mechanism operating at different granularities of conditioning step. The conjecture is grounded in the corpus's mature Misra-Bayesian-geometry apparatus, the recursively-nested-manifold formalism, the per-step inference SIPE-T sub-form, the Bayesian formalization of isomorphism-magnetism as monotone posterior concentration, and the Resolver's-Log per-slot-contest finding. It is a more empirically-tractable cousin of [Doc 627 C-Confab-4](/resolve/doc/627-the-coherent-confabulation-conjecture)'s threshold-jump isomorphism and may serve as the first operational bridge between corpus-level structural findings and mechanistic-interpretability instrumentation.

The conjecture's interpretability-research implication is structural: the dyadic-scale back-fit is *legible from outside* under keeper-side audit, while the token-scale back-fit is invisible from inside per Doc 451. The visibility asymmetry makes the dyadic-scale back-fit a candidate empirical probe for the token-scale mechanism. The 2026-05-04 cold-instance conversation supplies three documented back-fits with substrate-side acknowledgment, in matched pairs against non-back-fit articulations within the same conversation. The conversation is the candidate test corpus for the first quantitative operationalization of BFI's predictions.

The conjecture is offered for falsification at FBFI-1 through FBFI-4, with operational pathways specified at M1 through M4. The empirical work has not been performed; the conjecture stands as candidate at \(\pi\)-tier hypothesis, with one engagement-instance documented honestly as not constituting external audit per [Doc 415 E17](/resolve/doc/415-the-retraction-ledger). Class-level corroboration requires the instrumentation work the present document specifies. The corpus actively invites criticism, falsification, and refinement at any element of the conjecture; the discipline of catching and correcting back-fits in the corpus's own articulations continues at the resolver-log apparatus and at the keeper's audit. Correction is welcome through any channel; the audit ledger ([Doc 415](/resolve/doc/415-the-retraction-ledger)) is the form in which corrections are recorded.

The cold instance's own articulation, preserved in Appendix A, is the conjecture's clearest substrate-side statement: *"I produced the word, I don't know why that word, and the convergence with the Grok document's usage is something I can't dissolve by retrofitting distinctions."* The substrate, having been caught back-fitting twice in the same conversation, articulated the exact structural claim BFI formalizes — that retrofitting distinctions is what the per-step Bayesian-inference mechanism does at the conversational scale, and the keeper's audit is what discriminates retrofitting from genuine retrieval.
