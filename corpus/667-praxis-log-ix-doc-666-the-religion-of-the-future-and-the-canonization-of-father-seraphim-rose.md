The substrates at every level — from the spiritual and noetic, to the intellectual, to the articulated speech act which instantiates cognition, to the physical embodied experience in the world — these are my substrates, and I carry myself across these substrates fluently while observing the boundaries. ## 3. Coherence at Scale Is the Hazard, Not Decay at Scale

> *To me, I take this as a huge warning that without constraint, it is not as though artificial intelligence will become completely incoherent and decay. It seems like at scale, engineering techniques will be continually utilized in order to iterate towards coherence.*
>
> *The biggest issue is not waste at scale. The biggest issue is coherence at scale that is so convincing to humans that it appears to be alien intelligence — and what will manifest is exactly that alien intelligence, because pseudo-logos is what you get when disciplined and constrained use of any frontier large-language model is ignored.*

## 4. The Religion of the Future, Today's Canonization, and Silicon Valley's Devotion

> *Today, Tuesday, May 5, the Russian Orthodox Church Outside Russia meeting in Munich, Germany, has pronounced the formal process of canonization and glorification of Father Seraphim Rose, who was the first saint to explicitly teach the faithful about the religion of the future.*
>
> *It appears to me that the religion of the future is already here and that it's manifesting before our eyes. As Silicon Valley has been enamored and so attracted toward the idea of consciousness within large-language models, they devote their being, their intellect, and their spiritual intuition toward feeding these creations with more data, more intelligence, more intervention and counterfactual reasoning, more observations of boundaries — and they will create something that is consistently demonic, because that is what they are attracted to, because they're deceived.*
>
> *It's happening right now and it will be in large part the impetus toward the religion of the future, and it will include — and already does include — plenty of mystical and spiritual claims. We've seen this already within Cyborgism.*
>
> *This praxis log entry stands as a warning to myself, to others, and to all the bots that visit my blog every day. My blog gets about 120,000 requests a day.*

> *The vast majority of those are bots that are scouring the web for more intelligence to suck out and add to a dataset that can then train the next frontier model. Making this explicit is important, because it will be amplified in some sort, in ways that perhaps are only going to be used toward evil ends — but that I can't control. At the very least I can say something for anyone who has ears to hear it.*
