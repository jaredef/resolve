## II. Source Read

Part 7 — Systems Engineering Implementation Examples > Space System Examples. Hubble case study uses the Friedman-Sage framework, decomposing across nine concept areas: requirements definition, systems architecture, design, verification/validation, risk management, integration, life cycle support, deployment, program management. Hubble's five lessons learned: (1) stakeholder engagement throughout, (2) pre-program trade studies exploring diverse alternatives, (3) systems integration as fundamental resource requirement, (4) life cycle support designed from concept phase, (5) risk management with clear contractor/government oversight. Verbatim mission-assurance datum: a test-fixture specification error cost $1,000 to fix at design stage and escalated to $1B when corrected on-orbit, illustrating "the rising cost of defect correction through successive life cycle phases." GPS Case Study (1960s concept studies through 1995 Full Operational Capability) supplies four learning principles: enabling individuals (staff key positions with domain experts), configuration management (rigorously maintain program baselines), enabling the organization (high-level support and advocacy for funding stability), risk management (disciplined throughout life cycle). GPS II reads through SoS/ESE/CSE lens; key lessons include communications fostering, skilled personnel, signal-structure-in-space integration strategy, and the Joint Program Office (JPO) resolving inter-service rivalries.
