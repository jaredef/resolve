## II. Source Read

FDA Design Controls binding: "21 CFR 820.30 (Quality Systems Regulation, Subpart C Design Controls)" contains "requirements similar to ISO 13485" and "maps directly to ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 and the INCOSE SE Handbook." Design Control activities enumerated: design and development planning, design input (stakeholder and system requirements), design output (design definition and implementation), design review and assessment, design verification and validation, design transfer and transition, design changes and configuration management, design history file documentation. ISO standards enumerated: ISO 13485 (quality management for medical device manufacturers, aligns with FDA design controls), ISO 14971 (risk management; "users and patients are the center of risk analysis rather than technical or business risks" — risk traceability between hazards, mitigations, design inputs, outputs is critical for regulatory clearance), ISO 62366-1 (usability engineering; "analyze, specify, develop and evaluate the usability of a medical device as it relates to safety"), IEC 62304 (software life cycle management for medical devices as cyber-physical), ISO 21827 (privacy and security for connected medical systems).
