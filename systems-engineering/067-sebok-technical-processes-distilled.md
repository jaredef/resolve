## II. Source Read

System realization comprises four technical processes: System Implementation (creating system elements), System Integration (combining disparate elements), System Verification (alignment with requirements and architecture), System Validation (confirming the system meets stakeholder needs). These activities "are performed concurrently, iteratively and recursively depending on the selected life cycle model." The Vee model is referenced as illustrative: left-side definition activities, right-side realization activities. Upstream technical processes (per ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023): Business or Mission Analysis, Stakeholder Needs and Requirements Definition, System Requirements Definition, Architecture Definition, Design Definition, System Analysis. Downstream: Operation, Maintenance, Disposal. The full ISO 15288 technical process set numbers fourteen; SEBoK's organization groups them into Definition, Realization, and Deployment-and-Use clusters. Position: Part 3 SE and Management.

## III. Structural Read

**Form A (universal-sibling lattice, Doc 572 Appendix D).** The fourteen ISO 15288 technical processes are candidate universal-sibling lattice at the technical-process rung. Each process binds every life-cycle engagement universally as an aspect (every engagement performs requirements AND architecture AND verification, even if compressed); the discriminator is process-aspect, not rung-of-application. The case is strong: ISO 15288 is explicit that all processes are "performed throughout the life cycle as needed." Cluster A membership reaches eight (after specialty engineering at seven). The taxonomy itself is keeper-authored content the corpus accepts; the structure is universal-sibling.

**Form I (pin-art / temporal-concurrency, Doc 270 / Doc 572 Appendix C).** "Performed concurrently, iteratively and recursively depending on the selected life cycle model" is the canonical Doc 572 Appendix C statement. Each technical process is a pin-set; the substrate flows through all simultaneously; the lattice records the integration. The Vee model is one geometry of this lattice; spiral and agile are others. Cluster I membership extends; this is the pin-art form's most general SEBoK articulation.

**Form D (co-production at sub-rungs, Doc 573).** System Verification and System Validation are paired co-production processes (SE-029 already established Validation as canonical co-production; Verification is the substrate-side anchor of the same paired pulverization, Doc 445 Refinement A). The four realization processes form two co-production dyads: Implementation-Integration produces the substrate; Verification-Validation produces the conformance verdict.

**Form F (pulverization, Doc 445).** Verification-Validation is the canonical paired-V&V two-anchor pulverization at the technical-process rung. $T_I$ as verification anchor (alignment with internal specification); $T_E$ as validation anchor (alignment with external stakeholder need). Cluster F membership extends; the paired structure is now reachable through three SEBoK pages (594, 600, 633).

**Form C (architectural school, Doc 538).** The Vee, spiral, agile, and incremental schools all organize the same fourteen processes differently. The processes are the substrate; the school is the keeper-side discipline imposing sequence and concurrency. School-composition is structural at the technical-process rung; school-borrowing is observable (agile absorbs verification rigor from Vee).
