## II. Source Read

Lead authors Bud Lawson and Ricardo Pineda; contributing Rick Adcock. Part 4 — Applications of Systems Engineering. Verbatim definitions: product is "an artifact that is created by some person or by some process such as a manufacturing process, software source code compilation"; product system is "the combination of end products and the enabling products for those end products" (ANSI/EIA 632-2003); product realization system is the resources applied to conceive, develop, produce, test, and deploy the product system into its operational environment; product sustainment system is the services and enabling products needed to keep a deployed product operational throughout its lifecycle. The New Product Development Process (NPDP) encompasses two overlapping activities: SE (concept generation, design/development, deployment) and market development (research, analysis, acceptance, adoption rates). Considerations span manufacturability, logistics, quality, standards compliance, customer support. Adjacent carriers: *Service Systems Engineering*, *Enterprise Systems Engineering*, *Life-Cycle Costing*, *Whole-Life Value Engineering*. Standards: ANSI/EIA 632-2003; PLM-vendor de facto standards (Siemens Teamcenter, PTC Windchill, Dassault ENOVIA) carry substantive PLM-tooling articulation.
