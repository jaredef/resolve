## II. Source Read

Trade studies aim to "define, measure, and assess shareholder and stakeholder value to facilitate the decision maker's search for an alternative that represents the best balance of competing objectives." The ten-step process: framing and tailoring, developing objectives and measures, generating creative alternatives (morphological boxes), deterministic assessment, synthesizing results (value tables, value functions), probabilistic analysis (uncertainty, Monte Carlo), risk and sensitivity analysis, improving alternatives, communicating tradeoffs, presenting recommendations. MODA (Multiple Objective Decision Analysis) mathematically synthesizes competing priorities; the additive value model weights each measure's contribution. The "how much less would you accept to achieve how much more" framing quantifies tradeoffs across competing objectives. Five primary decision dimensions: Performance, Growth Potential, Schedule, Development & Procurement Costs, Sustainment Costs. Stakeholder value scatter plots visualize the dimensions.
