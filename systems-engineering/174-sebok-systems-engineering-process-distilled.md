## II. Source Read

Both *Systems Engineering Process* and *Applying the Systems Engineering Process* return 404. Process Concepts page supplies: "Systems engineering relies on a well-defined set of processes that guide the development of complex systems along the whole life cycle." Processes "are not meant to be executed in a serial, sequential fashion." Three execution mechanisms: Concurrency (processes operating simultaneously), Iteration (repeated cycles of process execution), Recursion (processes applied at multiple organizational levels). Standards binding: "ISO/IEC/IEEE 24774 standardizes process descriptions to ensure clarity, consistency, and comparability." Page structure: index referencing two sub-articles — *Process Description* and *Process Concurrency, Iteration, and Recursion*. Page itself "functions primarily as an index and introduction to these topics, with the actual detailed content residing in the linked articles."
