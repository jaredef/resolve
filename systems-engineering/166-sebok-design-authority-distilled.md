## II. Source Read

The target page does not exist. *Systems Engineering Organizational Strategy* supplies the canonical governance-rung quote: "SE governance is the process and practice through which a business puts in place the decision rights that enable SE to deliver as much business value as possible." Design authority is named as one of the matters SE governance addresses: "SE governance establishes the framework and responsibility for managing issues such as design authority, funding and approvals, project initiation and termination." The article does not name specific role titles (Chief Engineer, Technical Authority, Lead Systems Engineer) explicitly; rather it advocates "tailoring governance structures to organizational context rather than prescribing specific role titles." The structural instrument named is the RACI matrix: "It is good practice to capture the assignment of people and their roles and responsibilities in the form of the Responsible, Accountable, Consult, Inform (RACI) matrix." The emphasis is on clear assignment of decision rights and accountability regardless of formal titles.

Cluster C's role-instantiation worked example is now a vertical-thread worked example.

**Cluster K (virtue constraints, Doc 314), V3-as-procedure-binding at the RACI matrix.** The RACI matrix is V3 in procedural form: the matrix binds accountability against the rankism risk that responsibility-without-accountability is exercised (a participant has decision rights without bearing the consequences) and the complacency risk that accountability-without-responsibility is exercised (a senior holder is accountable but lacks the substrate-knowledge to exercise the rights). RACI's R/A/C/I four-fold partition itself is universal-sibling at the role-rung. Cluster K density grows; SE-166 supplies a governance-rung sub-mode of V3-as-procedure-binding.

**Cluster A (universal-sibling lattice, Doc 572 Appendix D), RACI four-fold lattice.** The Responsible / Accountable / Consult / Inform partition is a universal-sibling lattice at the role-rung. Each of the four binds every governance engagement aspect-wise; the discriminator is responsibility-mode. N=4 axes; small but clean. Cluster A gains a role-mode-rung instance.
